County Cars Privacy Policy

How County Cars collects, uses, and protects personal data in connection with the County Cars App and our SEN transport service.

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how County Cars (2026) Ltd ("County Cars", "we", "us", "our"), registered office Tinsley Street, Tipton, England, DY4 7LH, processes personal data in connection with the County Cars App (the "App") and related services used to deliver home-to-school transport for children with special educational needs and disabilities ("SEND") under contract with the relevant Council (the "Council").

We are committed to handling personal data — including that of children, parents/carers, and our drivers and passenger assistants ("Staff") — lawfully, fairly, and transparently, in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation ("UK GDPR"), the Data Protection Act 2018 ("DPA 2018"), and the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003 ("PECR").

1. Who we are and our role

For account data relating to Parents/Carers and Staff (e.g. login credentials, app usage, notification preferences), County Cars acts as the data controller.

For data relating to children receiving transport, route information, and pickup/drop-off records, County Cars processes this data under contract with, and on the instructions of, the Council, which is typically the data controller (or joint controller with County Cars) for that data. Where the Council determines the purposes and means of processing, this Policy operates alongside — and does not replace — the Council's own privacy notice, which takes precedence for any conflict regarding data processed on the Council's behalf.

Our nominated contact for data protection queries is Anthony Wallace, reachable at info@countycars2026.com or by post at Tinsley Street, Tipton, England, DY4 7LH.

2. Who this Policy covers

  • Parents, carers, and guardians ("Parents") who use the App to receive updates about their child's transport.
  • Children and young people receiving transport ("Students"), whose data is entered and used by Parents, the Council, and County Cars staff — Students themselves are not expected to hold App accounts.
  • Drivers and Passenger Assistants engaged by County Cars ("Staff" or "Drivers"), who use the App to receive job/route information, submit compliance documents, and report incidents.
  • County Cars office staff and coordinators who administer the App.

3. Personal data we collect

3.1 Parents and Carers

  • Name, relationship to Student, contact details (phone, email, address), login credentials.
  • Communication history with County Cars (support requests, complaints, feedback).
  • App usage data: notification preferences, login timestamps, device type.

3.2 Students (children)

  • Name, date of birth, home/pickup address, school, year group, assigned route and vehicle.
  • Special educational needs, disabilities, medical, or behavioural information relevant to safe transport (e.g. mobility equipment, allergies, seating requirements, escort requirements) — this is "special category data" under Article 9 UK GDPR and is processed under stricter safeguards described in Section 5.
  • Pickup/drop-off timestamps and, where the vehicle is fitted with tracking equipment, the vehicle's real-time location while the student is being transported (we track the vehicle, not the child individually).
  • Records of any incident, near-miss, complaint, or safeguarding concern involving the student during transport.

3.3 Drivers and Passenger Assistants

  • Name, contact details, address, date of birth, next of kin/emergency contact.
  • Enhanced DBS certificate details (certificate number, issue date, and outcome), which is "criminal offence data" under Article 10 UK GDPR.
  • Driving licence details, PCO/private hire or taxi driver badge/licence number, MiDAS or equivalent SEND-transport training certificates, and other role-specific qualifications, together with their expiry/renewal dates.
  • Staff member's photograph and badge/ID card details, together with a live photo submitted for facial recognition purposes.
  • Right-to-work documentation and, where applicable, employment or subcontractor engagement records.
  • Vehicle assignment, shift/route logs, and — while on shift — the real-time GPS location of the vehicle the Driver is operating.
  • Incident, accident, and near-miss reports submitted via the App, including any photos, notes, or third-party details recorded at the scene.
  • Records of reminders sent regarding licence, DBS, or document renewal, and Staff responses to those reminders.

3.4 Technical data (all users)

  • Device identifiers, operating system and app version, IP address, crash and diagnostic logs, and push notification tokens.
  • We do not use the App to serve third-party advertising, and we do not sell personal data to any third party.

4. How and why we use personal data

We use personal data for the following purposes. Where required, the corresponding UK GDPR lawful basis is shown.

  • To plan, allocate, and operate safe transport routes for Students — performance of our contract with the Council / legitimate interests in safe operation.
  • To notify Parents of vehicle arrival, delay, or route changes — legitimate interests in providing the service Parents have signed up to receive, and consent for optional push notifications where required by PECR.
  • To assign Drivers and Passenger Assistants to routes, issue day-to-day task/schedule information, and manage vehicle allocation — performance of contract / legitimate interests.
  • To verify and track Drivers and Passenger Assistants' compliance (DBS status, licence and training expiry) and prompt timely renewal — legal obligation (safeguarding legislation and our contractual duties to the Council) and legitimate interests in safeguarding children.
  • To receive, investigate, and report incidents, accidents, or safeguarding concerns — legal obligation and vital interests where safety is at risk, and legitimate interests in resolving and learning from incidents.
  • To maintain accurate records for Council reporting, audit, and safeguarding compliance — legal obligation and public task.
  • To secure App accounts, prevent fraud or misuse, and maintain and improve the App — legitimate interests.

5. Special category and criminal offence data

Some of the data we process is subject to extra legal protection:

  • Health, disability, and SEN-related data about Students is special category data. We process it only to the extent necessary to provide safe transport (e.g. seating, mobility, medical needs), relying on the substantial public interest conditions in Schedule 1 of the DPA 2018 (in particular, conditions relating to safeguarding of children and individuals at risk) and, where relevant, explicit consent obtained by the Council or Parent.
  • DBS certificate and criminal offence data about Staff is processed under Article 10 UK GDPR and Schedule 1 DPA 2018 (employment, regulated activity, and safeguarding conditions), consistent with the DBS Code of Practice. We do not process this data on the basis of consent alone, because it is a condition of the safeguarding-critical role — see the separate Driver and Passenger Assistant Data Processing & Document Retention Acknowledgement signed by Staff on joining.
  • Access to special category and criminal offence data within County Cars is restricted to staff who need it to perform their role (e.g. coordinators, compliance, fleet), and is not shared with Parents or other Drivers and Passenger Assistants.

6. Location and vehicle tracking

Vehicles are fitted with GPS tracking used to:

(a) provide Parents with estimated arrival times and arrival notifications;

(b) allow coordinators to monitor routes in real time for safety and operational purposes; and

(c) provide an accurate record in the event of an incident, complaint, or Council audit.

Parents can see the live status of their own child's journey (e.g. "on route", "arrived") but cannot see other Students' journeys or continuous vehicle tracking beyond what is necessary to inform them of their child's transport. Drivers are tracked while logged into the App during working hours; tracking data is not used to monitor Drivers outside of assigned shifts.

Phone-based location tracking for active trips

For drivers and passenger assistants, the App may also collect precise location data from the crew member's phone while an assigned trip is active. This is used to provide live office tracking, parent arrival information, and journey safety and incident records when the vehicle is not equipped with a separate GPS tracker.

Phone tracking is designed to start only after:

  • A driver or passenger assistant has an assigned trip that has been started;
  • The person has seen the in-app location disclosure and selected Continue; and
  • Location permission has been granted by the device operating system.

The in-app disclosure explains that precise location data is collected to provide live office tracking, parent arrival information, and journey safety and incident records. On Android, the disclosure states that location may be collected even when the app is closed or not in use while an assigned trip is active, with a visible notification. On iOS, the disclosure states that location may be collected while the app is in the background during an active trip, and that closing the app fully may pause tracking until the app is opened again.

Selecting Not now, declining location permission, logging out, completing a trip, or a trip synchronisation failure is designed to prevent or stop phone tracking and clear queued location records. County Cars does not use phone location tracking to monitor drivers or passenger assistants outside assigned active trips.

The phone location records may include:

  • Precise latitude and longitude.
  • Time of the location record.
  • Accuracy, speed, heading, and whether the location service reports the device as moving.
  • Odometer-related information supplied by the location service.
  • Battery level and charging status.
  • Location-provider status, including whether location, GPS, or network providers are enabled.
  • Device platform, operating-system version, device model, app version, and installation identifier.
  • The assigned route-session identifier.

The App sends phone location records to the County Cars backend through an authenticated request while phone tracking is enabled for an active trip. We do not sell phone location data.

The App does not request Android Physical Activity / Activity Recognition permission or iOS Motion permission. It does not use Motion or Physical Activity sensor data as a separate tracking purpose.

Location data retention

Phone-based GPS location records stored on the server are retained for 90 days after receipt and are automatically deleted by the scheduled retention process.

The mobile app is configured to retain unsent native location records locally on the device for up to three days while tracking is active. This local device queue is designed to be cleared when tracking stops. Local device retention is separate from server-side retention and does not determine how long previously uploaded records are stored.

Vehicle live-location data from Samsara is maintained in the vehicles_realtime table, which stores the latest reported location for each vehicle rather than accumulating a continuous history of location records. Any separately stored historical vehicle telematics records are subject to their applicable retention process.

7. Who we share personal data with

We may share personal data with:

  • The Council, as required by our contract, including for audit, safeguarding, and funding-verification purposes.
  • The Disclosure and Barring Service, where required for DBS applications, renewals, or the DBS Update Service.
  • Regulatory and law enforcement bodies, local authority safeguarding teams, and emergency services, where necessary to protect a child or adult at risk, to comply with a legal obligation, or to investigate a reported incident.
  • Our insurers and legal advisers, in connection with accident, incident, or liability claims.
  • IT and hosting providers, app development and support contractors, and analytics/crash-reporting providers who process data on our behalf under written data processing agreements.
  • A prospective buyer or successor of the business, in the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to appropriate confidentiality safeguards.

The App uses the following services in operating the App:

  • Supabase for authentication, application database services, realtime services, backend requests, and file storage.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging for push notifications. The App sends the push-notification token, installation identifier, platform, and authenticated account context to a Firebase Cloud Function so notifications can be delivered.
  • Google Maps to display maps and vehicle or trip locations within the App.
  • Sentry, where enabled, for technical error and performance reporting.
  • Samsara telematics, where a vehicle is equipped with a Samsara tracker. County Cars may use vehicle-location information supplied through that telematics integration to operate live vehicle tracking. The App does not send staff-phone GPS records to Samsara.

If you choose to open directions in another navigation application, such as Google Maps or Waze, that provider will handle the destination information under its own privacy policy.

We do not sell personal data. We do not share Student or Staff special category or criminal offence data with any third party for marketing purposes.

8. International transfers

Personal data is hosted and processed within the United Kingdom / European Economic Area wherever possible. Where a service provider processes data outside the UK, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement/Addendum or a finding of adequacy, before any transfer takes place.

9. Retention

We retain personal data for no longer than necessary for the purposes it was collected, and in line with the Council's contractual and statutory retention requirements. As a general guide:

  • Driver and Passenger Assistant certificates, badges and compliance documents: retained for the duration of the Driver's/Passenger Assistant's engagement and thereafter for six years, where necessary to support contractual, safeguarding, compliance, audit, insurance and legal requirements, or such other period as required or instructed by the Council. The retention period is subject to the Council's applicable records retention schedule and will be reviewed periodically.
  • Incident and accident reports: retained for six years from the date of the incident to cover insurance, legal claim, and safeguarding review periods.
  • Phone-based GPS location records: retained on the server for 90 days after receipt, after which they are automatically deleted by the scheduled retention process.
  • Vehicle live-location data: maintained as the latest reported location per vehicle in the vehicles_realtime table, with new location updates replacing the previous live-location record. Any separately stored historical vehicle telematics records are subject to their applicable retention process.
  • App account and login data: deleted or anonymised within twelve months of account closure.

Where an exact retention period is dictated by the Council's contract or policy, that period governs and will be confirmed to affected individuals on request.

10. Children's privacy

We recognise that Students are children, some of whom are particularly vulnerable due to their SEND. We collect only the data necessary to provide safe transport, obtained via the Council and/or Parents rather than directly from children, and we do not permit children to hold independent App accounts.

11. Security

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including access controls restricting special category and criminal offence data to authorised staff, encryption of data in transit, secure hosting, and regular review of who has access to what. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we take reasonable and appropriate steps to protect data and will notify affected individuals and/or the ICO without undue delay in the event of a notifiable personal data breach, in line with our legal obligations.

12. Automated decision-making

Route allocation may be assisted by software (e.g. route optimisation), but no decision that produces a legal or similarly significant effect on a Student, Parent, or Driver is made by fully automated means without human review.

13. Your rights

Subject to legal exemptions, you have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal data.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request erasure.
  • Request restriction of processing.
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Request data portability.
  • Withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent. This does not affect processing carried out before withdrawal, and does not apply to processing required by law or contract, such as DBS checks.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 1. We will respond within one month, in accordance with the UK GDPR. If you are not satisfied with our response, you have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or 0303 123 1113.

14. Cookies and similar technologies

The App may use strictly necessary technologies to keep you logged in and to remember preferences, and diagnostic/crash-reporting tools to help us fix bugs. We do not use advertising cookies or trackers. Where any non-essential technology is used, we will ask for your consent in accordance with PECR.

15. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, the law, or our contract with the Council. Material changes will be notified within the App or by email before they take effect. The "Last updated" date at the top of this Policy shows when it was last revised.
